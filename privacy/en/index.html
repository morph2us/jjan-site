Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026 (revised)

Also available: 한국어 · EN · 日本語 · 中文 · ES

In short: you use Jjan with Sign in with Apple, and all of your records are stored only on your device and in your own iCloud. The developer runs no servers and cannot see what or how much you drank. Only non-personalized ads are shown.

1. Information We Collect and Store

The information you enter in the app, listed below, is stored on your device and is never sent to the developer's servers (the developer does not operate any servers).

  • Profile information (sex, age band, body type, usual intake, flush response) — used to personalize the light calculation
  • Drink logs (drink type, number of glasses, time), session plans, and morning condition answers
  • Voice files you record for the Escape feature

2. Account and iCloud Backup

  • Using the Service requires a Sign in with Apple account. The app receives an anonymous user identifier issued by Apple, a name (entered by you or provided by Apple), and the email address Apple provides (you can choose a private relay address — used only for display on the account screen). We do not collect any other information, such as your contacts.
  • Your records, profile, plans, and profile photo are backed up to your own iCloud private database (CloudKit). This space is operated by Apple and belongs to your Apple account, and the developer cannot access it. Apple's handling is governed by apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • Signing out stops the backup; the records on your device remain as they are.
  • Deleting your account (from the account screen in the app) permanently erases the iCloud backup and all records and recordings on your device. This cannot be undone.

3. How Data Is Processed and Kept

  • All calculations (intoxication estimates, report generation) happen on your device. Estimates are for reference only and are never sent anywhere.
  • Data is kept under iOS device encryption.
  • Depending on your device's iCloud Backup settings, it may be included in your personal iCloud device backup.

4. Notifications

Water reminders, pacing alerts, and morning report notifications are all local notifications. No push servers are used.

5. Payments

Premium subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple App Store billing. The developer never receives your payment details; the app only checks the anonymized transaction status Apple provides (whether a subscription is active).

6. Advertising

If you are not a Premium user, a non-personalized ad from Google AdMob may be shown when you start a session. Jjan does not track you for ad targeting (no App Tracking Transparency request is made); Google may process limited data such as device information to serve ads. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy. Premium subscribers see no ads.

7. Third Parties and Analytics

To improve the app, we collect anonymous usage statistics (no personal identification) — using a privacy-first analytics tool (Aptabase), which sends only aggregate-level information about which screens and features are used. No personal identifiers, advertising IDs (IDFA), or drink log contents are included, and it is not used for cross-app tracking. Apart from the (non-personalized) ads above, we use no other tracking tools and share no personal information with third parties. If this ever changes, we will update this policy and give notice in the app.

8. Children's Privacy

Jjan is an app for users at or above the legal drinking age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
